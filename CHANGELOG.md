# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — Research artifact release

This release combines the original July 2026 experiment with a September 2026 reproducibility and documentation update. The historical `bench27_runs.json`, `bench27_cost.json` and `opus_runs.json` retain their original bytes.

- Lead with the AIS3 2026 AI Track Best Project Award and linked recognition records.
- Provide an English landing page, a complete Traditional Chinese version, paired research briefs and claim–evidence ledgers.
- Add source-grounded related-work positioning, four-member software citations and a reproducible descriptive outcome figure.
- Rebuild the 803-attempt result snapshot offline; publish a sanitized audit covering 30 local logs, seven exclusions and superseded runs.
- Centralize new-run settings and separate new experiment outputs from historical evidence.
- Fix assistant-only extraction, custom flag matching and archived task paths; add audited export with complete-run selection.
- Restrict six custom service networks to internal connectivity; upstream and historical network conditions remain separate.
- Add regression tests, CI, contribution/security guidance, provenance notes, and a public archive suitable for offline review.

### Scope

This is a software/data artifact, not a peer-reviewed paper or a new state-of-the-art claim. Exact presentation process-score matrices, model revisions, frontier reconciliation and full container execution remain documented evidence gaps. Third-party assets retain upstream terms; project MIT covers original work only.
