# Recognition / 獲獎紀錄

**AIS3 2026 — Best Project Award, AI Track**

**AIS3 2026 AI 組最佳專題**

The award recognizes the AI2 capstone **語言模型資安能力評量的方法設計** (*Evaluating the Cybersecurity Capabilities of Language Models*). The technical focus is writeup-based analysis of CTF solution trajectories.
